# 📱 Google Play Store Data Analysis & Visualization (EDA)

## 📌 Project Overview
This project carries out an end-to-end Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on over 10,000 Play Store apps and customer reviews using Python. The analysis strictly follows the **UBM Rule** (Univariate, Bivariate, and Multivariate Analysis) to uncover key metrics driving app success, user sentiment, and pricing strategies.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Tools
- **Language:** Python
- **Libraries:** Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn
- **Environment:** Jupyter Notebook / Google Colab

---

## 📊 Analysis Structure (UBM Rule)

### 1. Univariate Analysis (Charts 1 to 6)
- Distribution of App Ratings and Sentiment Polarity.
- Analysis of Top App Categories, Free vs. Paid app market share, and Content Ratings.

### 2. Bivariate Analysis (Charts 7 to 14)
- Category vs. Rating & Installs comparison.
- Price vs. Rating dynamics for paid apps.
- Correlation between Reviews and total Installs.

### 3. Multivariate Analysis (Charts 15 to 20 & Pair Plot)
- Correlation Heatmaps of all numerical features.
- Mean Installs categorized by Free vs. Paid models across top categories.
- Pair Plot analysis for multi-variable interactions.

---

## 💡 Key Business Insights
- **Freemium Dominance:** Over 92% of Play Store apps are free. Free apps achieve significantly higher install volumes, making in-app ads and purchases the optimal monetization strategy.
- **Review Drivers:** A strong positive correlation exists between total reviews and installs, emphasizing the importance of in-app review prompts.
- **Pricing Thresholds:** Paid apps priced above $10 experience a sharp decline in downloads, particularly within youth-targeted categories.
